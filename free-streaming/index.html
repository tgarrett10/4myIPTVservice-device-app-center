Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Is there a monthly subscription?

No recurring television fee is charged by us for this free-app configuration package. You pay once for the device, setup and agreed support. Internet service is required and your internet provider may charge separately.

Why not just buy the device and install apps myself?

You absolutely can. Our service is for customers who prefer to skip researching apps, setting up the home screen and getting familiar with the options. You're paying for convenience and configuration, not exclusive access to free content.

Are all of these apps completely free?

Each featured service has an authorized free offering, though some also have optional paid tiers, accounts or ads. Kanopy and hoopla may require a participating library account. App compatibility varies by model and region.

Will I get every cable channel and live sports event?

No. These legitimate free streaming options provide varied programming but are not an exact replacement for a full cable package. Specific networks, sports events, broadcasts, local stations and titles are not guaranteed.

Can you add my existing paid services?

We can help you locate and install supported official apps for subscriptions you already maintain. You remain responsible for your own accounts and subscription charges. Paid subscriptions are not included in our free-streaming package.
