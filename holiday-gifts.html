Anywhere in the continental U.S. Shipping is an additional $15 per recipient address using two-day FedEx service. Holiday gift orders ship December 16. Carrier delivery timing can vary.

Yes. Contact us for multi-device rates, availability, and local pickup arrangements. Every device requires recipient contact details; an address is required for shipped gifts.

You are identified as the gift giver on the To/From tag and holiday message. We prepare and ship the package on your behalf, and include our direct contact information for any setup questions.
