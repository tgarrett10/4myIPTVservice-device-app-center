1. Use your IPTV Smarters App

Refresh Live TV, Movies, and Series when you need the latest listings. The refresh control may vary by app version.

  • Live TV: Choose a category, select a channel, then open the preview to watch full screen.
  • Favorites: Press and hold the center button on supported items to save your most-watched channels, movies, and shows.
  • Movies & Series: Select All Movies or All Series, then search for the title.
  • Guide: Use the electronic program guide when available. Channel options depend on your subscription.

Missing program information? Refresh your TV guide.

  1. Open Live TV in the IPTV Smarters App.
  2. Select the three-dot options menu in the upper-right corner.
  3. Open Settings, then select EPG—Electronic Program Guide.
  4. Select the refresh/update option. When it shows Updating, allow it to finish before returning to your channels.

Menu names may vary by app version. If you can’t find the option or the guide remains empty, contact support.

Looking for channels in another language or region? Additional channel options may be available through your IPTV service. Contact me on WhatsApp or Telegram to discuss what you’d like to watch and whether your package needs updating.
Keep your login private. When asking for account help, include your service username. Do not send your password unless it is needed for an agreed setup step.

2. Open My Support page on your TV

Keep this guide open on your phone or computer while you use your TV remote:

  1. On your streaming device, open Downloader—the orange app with a white “D” and a downward-pointing arrow. If it isn’t on your Home screen, look under Apps or See all apps.
  2. Navigate all the way to the top-left corner and open Downloader’s menu.
  3. Select Favorites, then My Support page.
  4. If it isn’t listed, select Home from Downloader’s menu. Enter 2732515 in the address/search box and select Go.
  5. Once the support page opens on your TV, choose Apps & Updates for app installation instructions, or the guide or troubleshooting option for help.
One starting point: Use My Support page in Downloader whenever you need device help or app updates.

3. Optional apps & updates

If you’re interested in additional sideloaded apps for movies and series, use Downloader on your TV streaming device. Follow the steps in section 2 to open My Support page, then select Apps & Updates to open the App Library on your TV. Find the app you want and follow its installation instructions there. To update an existing app, use the same steps. Some updates require removing the old version first.

  • Flix Vision: Version 4.0.0r-2 is the newly posted improved release.
  • FilmPlus: Uninstall the old FilmPlus version first, then install version 2.5.2.
  • Reezn: Install the update over the existing app. No uninstall is needed. Find its update in Apps & Updates through Downloader on your TV.

These optional apps are separate from your paid IPTV subscription. Their availability and updates can change.

Install on your TV: Use this guide for instructions. Download and install apps through Downloader on your streaming device.

4. If something is not working

  1. Close and reopen the affected app.
  2. Check that the device is connected to the internet.
  3. Restart the streaming device and try again.
  4. Open My Support page in Downloader on your TV, then select Apps & Updates. Read any posted notice or app-specific instructions before reinstalling.

If you use Background Apps & Process List, close unused viewing apps when needed. Avoid changing unfamiliar system settings.

Still need help? Tell me your service username, device model, app name, and what happens. A picture of the error can help.
