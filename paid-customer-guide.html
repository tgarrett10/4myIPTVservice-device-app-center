Your paid service

Use the IPTV Smarters App for your paid service. It is where you can watch Live TV, Movies, and Series included with your package.

Live TV

Choose a category, select a channel, then open the preview to watch full screen. Use the guide when it is available.

Movies & Series

Open All Movies or All Series, then use Search to find a title. You can save favorites on supported items.

Keep it current

If listings or program information are missing, refresh the app or its guide before contacting support.

Keep your login private. When you ask for help, send your service username—not your password—unless a specific setup step requires it.

Open the Support Page on your TV

This is the one place to go for device help, app instructions, and current updates.

Open Downloader
Find the orange app with the white “D” and down arrow. It may be under Apps or See all apps.
Open Favorites
Go to the top-left menu in Downloader, then select Favorites.
Select 4MyIPTV Service Support Page
Use this saved favorite whenever you need help or an update.
If it is not in Favorites
Choose Home in Downloader, enter code 2732515, then select Go.
Remember this: For apps or device help, open Downloader → Favorites → 4MyIPTV Service Support Page.

Apps & updates

On your TV, open the Support Page, then choose Apps & Updates. Read the note under each app before installing or updating it.

Movie Box TV

Version 1.1.9 — install the update over the existing app. No uninstall is needed. Afterward, you may delete the downloaded APK file.

FilmPlus

Uninstall the previous FilmPlus version first, then install version 2.5.2.

Flix Vision

Version 4.0.0r-2 is the current listed release. Follow the instructions shown beside the download.

Reezn

Install the update over the existing app. No uninstall is needed.

Optional apps are separate from your paid service. Their availability, features, and updates can change. Install them only through the Support Page on your TV and follow the posted instructions.

Try this first if something is not working

  1. Close and reopen the affected app.
  2. Confirm that the streaming device is connected to the internet.
  3. Restart the streaming device.
  4. Open the Support Page in Downloader and read the current app-specific note before reinstalling anything.

If you contact support, please include your service username, device model, app name, and a brief description or photo of what you see.

Need help or ready to renew?
