First-time device setup
  1. Connect the preconfigured device to your home Wi-Fi.
  2. Open your service app and confirm it works.
  3. We recommend adding your own Google account so you can manage your personal Google services and applications. The device was initially prepared using the 4MyIPTV Service account.

Do not factory-reset the device or remove accounts until you understand the effect on your setup. Contact customer support if you need assistance.

How to update an app
  1. Open Downloader on your device.
  2. Use the menu in the upper-left corner and select Favorites.
  3. Choose the saved 4MyIPTV Service Support Page.
  4. Find your app under Recent Updates or All Apps & Utilities. Review the version and any app-specific instructions.
  5. For optional app updates, our recommended approach is to uninstall the old version through device Settings before installing the new one. Save any necessary login information first: uninstalling may erase settings, favorites or login details. Check app-specific guidance and contact customer support before removing the primary service app.
  6. Select the update, download it, and complete installation. Select Done to return to Downloader.
  7. Select Delete to remove the installer file, then select Delete again to confirm. This does not uninstall the application.
  8. Repeat for other apps as needed, then press Home or Back to finish.

Keep device security features enabled. For the primary service app, contact customer support before uninstalling, clearing its data, or resetting your device.

Service login and troubleshooting

Open IPTV Smarters Pro, choose the login method specified by customer support, and enter your service details exactly as supplied. Never send your password when requesting support.

  • Close and reopen the affected app.
  • Check Wi-Fi, restart the device, and restart the router if needed.
  • Run a speed test on the same device and network if playback is slow.
  • If a suspicious download or security warning appears, stop installation and contact customer support.
  • For an outdated optional app, read the current update note before reinstalling.

Support is provided for listed service and optional apps; applications installed from other sources may not be supported.

Refresh the Electronic Program Guide (EPG)

If channel listings are blank, outdated or show the wrong time in IPTV Smarters Pro, try these steps:

  1. Open IPTV Smarters Pro and return to its main screen.
  2. Select Install EPG or Update EPG if available. On some versions, the refresh option is under Settings or the Live TV guide menu.
  3. Allow the guide to finish updating. Return to Live TV and check the program listings again.
  4. If listings are still missing, close and reopen the app, check your internet connection, and confirm that your device's date, time and time zone are correct.
  5. If the guide remains blank, contact customer support. Guide listings depend on your service provider, and some channels may not have program information.

Tip: Menu names differ by app version. Do not clear app data, uninstall the primary service app or change your login information just to refresh the guide.

Reduce buffering or playback issues

  1. Try another channel or title to see whether the issue affects one stream or several.
  2. Close and reopen the app; restart your streaming device if needed.
  3. Check your Wi-Fi connection and run a speed test on the affected device. Restart your router if appropriate.
  4. If the problem continues, contact customer support with the app name, device model and affected channel or title.

Find and save favorite channels

  1. Open Live TV and use Search to find a channel.
  2. Highlight the channel and use the available favorite or Add to Favorites option.
  3. Open the Favorites category to find saved channels later. Controls vary by device and app version.
